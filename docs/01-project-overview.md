## 1. Purpose

This document establishes the foundational understanding of the LearnCS product. It defines what LearnCS is, why it exists, and what it aims to achieve. All subsequent discovery and definition documents reference this document as the source of product identity.

---

## 2. Product Summary

LearnCS is a Learning Operating System for Computer Science education.

It is not a course platform, not a video library, not a coding bootcamp, and not a learning management system.

LearnCS is a persistent, adaptive environment that manages the full lifecycle of a learner's Computer Science journey through a set of interconnected engines, not a collection of isolated features.

The product is designed to address the structural failures in how Computer Science is currently taught and learned, rather than offering incremental improvements to existing solutions.

---

## 3. Vision

To become the definitive environment in which people learn Computer Science, a system that understands what each learner knows, identifies what they need next, and connects their learning directly to professional competence.

---

## 4. Mission

To replace the fragmented, passive, and one-size-fits-all approach to CS education with an integrated system that models knowledge accurately, delivers learning adaptively, and produces professionals who are genuinely competent, not merely credentialed.

---

## 5. Core Product Philosophy

- **Knowledge over completion.** The system measures what a learner understands, not what they consumed.
- **Structure over content.** CS knowledge has an inherent structure. The product makes that structure navigable rather than delivering content linearly.
- **Engines over features.** The product is composed of interconnected systems that communicate with each other, not a list of independent features.
- **Signal over noise.** Every interaction produces meaningful feedback. The learner always knows where they stand.
- **Path over catalog.** The product provides a personalized, navigable path, not a searchable catalog of courses.

> *Requires Founder Decision: These principles are derived from prior discovery conversations. They must be explicitly reviewed and approved as the official Product Principles before they govern feature decisions.*

---

## 6. Primary Objectives

1. Model the actual knowledge state of each learner with accuracy, not proxies.
2. Externalize the prerequisite structure of Computer Science into a navigable system.
3. Provide tight, continuous feedback loops between learning and understanding.
4. Generate personalized learning paths based on real learner state and goals.
5. Connect learning outcomes directly to career readiness.

---

## 7. Expected Value

**For Learners:**
- A clear, personalized path from their current knowledge to their professional goal.
- Accurate awareness of what they know and what they need to learn next.
- Learning that adapts to their pace, gaps, and strengths.
- A direct connection between their learning progress and career opportunities.

**For the Market:**
- A product that addresses the root cause of CS education failure, not the symptoms.
- A defensible position in a market dominated by content platforms that compete on volume rather than learning outcomes.

---

## 8. Success Definition

> *Requires Founder Decision: Specific success metrics and KPIs have not been defined. The following are candidate dimensions for measuring success. Quantitative targets must be set by the Founder.*

Success should be measured across these dimensions:

- **Learning Effectiveness:** Learners demonstrably understand what the system reports they understand.
- **Learner Retention:** Learners engage with the system over sustained periods (months, not sessions).
- **Career Outcomes:** Learners who complete recommended paths achieve measurable career results.
- **Knowledge Accuracy:** The system's model of a learner's knowledge state correlates with actual performance.
